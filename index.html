Why Metafab

How it works

From first call to final clean-up.

Four straightforward steps, with one person answering your questions the whole way through.

  1. Free site survey

    We visit, measure properly and talk through options, glass and colours. No pressure and no charge.

  2. Written quote

    A fixed price with a clear breakdown, usually within 48 hours. Nothing starts until you say yes.

  3. Fabrication

    Your unit is made to size in our workshop and we agree an installation date that suits you.

  4. Install & handover

    Our fitters install, seal, test and clean up, then walk you through what's been done and the warranty.
