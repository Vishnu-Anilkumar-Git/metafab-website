The standard behind the photos

What you can't see in a finished photo.

A neat frame is the easy part. The difference is in the things that decide whether the job still looks right in ten years.

  • Correct fixings for the substrate. Thermal movement planned for, packers and shims used properly, fixings into the right material — not just the nearest brick.
  • Drainage and ventilation thought through. Weepholes, drainage paths and roof ventilation designed in, so water goes where it should.
  • Sealant that will last. Low-modulus, UV-stable sealants with correct joint sizes, not a smear of silicone over a gap.
  • Documented handover. Photos of the work as it went in, product data and your warranty, in one pack.
