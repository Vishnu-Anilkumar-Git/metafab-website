Before you ask

Common questions.

Is the survey and quote really free?

Yes. We survey, measure and quote at no charge and with no obligation. For larger commercial work we may ask for drawings or a site meeting first, but the quote itself is still free.

How long does a quote take?

Usually within 48 hours of the survey. Straight replacements are often quoted the same day if you can send photos and sizes.

How long is the wait for installation?

Typical aluminium windows and doors are 3–5 weeks from order, depending on colour and glass specification. Roofing is usually scheduled within 2–3 weeks, and emergency repairs sooner.

Do you do small jobs?

Yes — a single window, a leaking flashing or a section of guttering is fine. We'd rather fix the small thing properly than see it become a large one.

What warranty do I get?

Ten years on our workmanship, plus the manufacturer warranty on profiles, glass and roofing systems — typically 10–25 years depending on the product. Everything is written down in your handover pack.

Can you work around a business's opening hours?

Yes. Shopfronts and commercial glazing are usually installed early morning, evenings or over a weekend so you don't lose trading time.
