A workshop, a van, and twenty years of getting it right.

Metafab started as a two-man fabrication shop making windows and gates for local builders. We've grown, but the model hasn't changed: we make the thing, we fit the thing, and we answer the phone afterwards.

Our story

Started in a unit. Still in the same unit.

We began taking on aluminium work that other firms didn't want — awkward openings, listed buildings, roofs nobody could find the leak in. That's still most of what we do, and it's why builders and architects keep coming back with the difficult ones.

Today we cover aluminium fabrication and roofing under one roof, which means one point of contact instead of three trades blaming each other. If the roof meets the glazing, we're responsible for the join — and we're the ones who come back if it leaks.

Most of our work now comes from repeat customers and recommendations from people we've worked for. We'd rather keep it that way than chase volume.

Straight answers, even when they cost us the job. If a repair is the sensible option instead of a replacement, we'll say so.

What we stand for

Four things we don't compromise on.

Quotes you can compare

Itemised, in writing, with the glass specification and the lead time stated. If two quotes differ, you'll be able to see exactly why.

Dates we keep

We don't book more work than the workshop can make or the fitters can install. If a date has to move, you'll know early — not on the morning.

Materials that last

Materials & warranty

Specified properly, guaranteed in writing.

What goes into the job decides how long it lasts. Here's what we use as standard, and what you get in writing when we finish.

  • Thermally broken aluminium profiles with polyamide thermal breaks, powder-coated to your chosen RAL colour.
  • A-rated double glazing as standard — triple glazing, acoustic or solar-control glass where the situation calls for it.
  • Stainless-steel fixings and correct packers, so nothing rusts or works loose behind the frame.
  • Single-ply and metal roofing systems from established manufacturers, installed to their published specification.
  • 10-year workmanship warranty, plus the full manufacturer guarantee on profiles, glass and roofing membranes.
