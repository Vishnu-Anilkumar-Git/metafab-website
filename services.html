Aluminium windows

Slim sightlines, more glass and no maintenance. We supply and fit thermally broken aluminium windows that suit both modern extensions and older properties — including heritage-style frames and flush casements where the look matters.

  • Casement, tilt-and-turn, fixed and top-hung openings
  • Double or triple glazed, A-rated, argon filled as standard
  • Powder-coated to any RAL colour, inside and out
  • Trickle vents and acoustic glazing available for busy roads

Sliding & bi-fold doors

Patio doors that actually slide. We fit lift-and-slide and bi-fold systems up to full wall width, with low thresholds for level access, so the inside and the garden feel like one space.

  • Lift-and-slide, inline sliding and bi-fold configurations
  • Flush and low-threshold options for wheelchair access
  • Double or triple track, with secure multi-point locking
  • Matching entrance, French and composite-style doors

Price up a door

Shopfronts & commercial glazing

Shopfronts, offices, workshops and industrial units. We measure to the millimetre, fabricate in our workshop and install outside trading hours so a refit doesn't cost you a day of takings.

  • Shopfronts, entrance screens and automatic doors
  • Curtain walling, window walls and structural glazing
  • Security glazing, shutters and laminated glass
  • Fascias, cills, trims and making good

Talk to us about a commercial fit-out

Roofing: metal, tile and flat

New roofs and full re-roofs, built with the right membrane, battens, ventilation and fixings — not just what you can see from the street. We work on pitched roofs, extensions, garages and commercial buildings.

  • Standing-seam and profiled metal roofing
  • Single-ply flat roofs and warm-deck build-ups
  • Slate, concrete tile and clay tile re-roofs
  • Fascias, soffits, guttering, downpipes and roofline

Book a roof survey

Roof repairs & maintenance

A leak is rarely where the stain is. We trace the actual entry point, fix the cause and photograph the work so you can see what was done — useful when you're selling, letting or claiming on insurance.

  • Leak tracing and emergency repairs
  • Flashing, valleys, chimney and parapet repairs
  • Flat roof patches and full membrane replacement
  • Gutter clearing, repairs and replacements

Report a leak or book a repair

Balustrades & glass railings

Balcony, stair and roof-terrace balustrades that meet current height and loading requirements. Frameless glass for the cleanest look, or posts and rails where a stronger line suits the building.

  • Frameless glass and glass-with-posts systems
  • Aluminium and steel handrails, powder-coated to match
  • Balcony, staircase, Juliet and roof-terrace applications
  • Structural fixings and load calculations documented

Ask about a balustrade

Carports, canopies & pergolas

Shelter for cars, bikes, bins or the back door. Designed to suit the house, built from aluminium that won't rot, rust or need repainting, and anchored properly to cope with wind loading.

  • Freestanding and wall-mounted carports
  • Glass, polycarbonate or solid aluminium roofs
  • Entrance canopies and commercial walkway shelters
  • Lounging pergolas with optional screens and lighting

Design a canopy or carport

Gates, fencing & security

Driveway gates and boundary screening fabricated to your opening, not cut down from a catalogue size. Manual or automated, with the safety sensors and controls done properly.

  • Sliding, swing and pedestrian gates
  • Slatted and privacy screening panels
  • Automation with safety edges and remote control
  • Matching bin stores, bike stores and enclosures
